Get help

If Fight Clock is not working as expected, open a support request on GitHub.

Open a support request

Include these details

  • your iPhone model and iOS version;
  • the Fight Clock version;
  • what you expected, what happened, and steps to reproduce it;
  • whether notifications were allowed and whether Silent Mode or Focus was enabled.

Do not include personal, medical, or confidential information in a public issue.
